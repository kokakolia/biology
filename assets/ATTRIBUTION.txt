Source geometry: BodyParts3D 4.0, PART-OF Wavefront OBJ archive.
Esophagus: FJ2563. Stomach: FJ2564. Pancreas: FJ2629 (parenchyma).
Liver: tissue segments FJ2409, FJ2816, FJ2818–FJ2824.
Small intestine: FJ2573–FJ2628. Large intestine: FJ2566–FJ2572.
Torso: exterior surface FJ2810 cropped at the neck, sides, and lower pelvis.
Body context (body-context.glb): uncut skin FJ2810, rib cage, cervical and thoracic
vertebrae, and bones of the shoulder girdles, arms and hands, selected from the
same PART-OF archive. The viewer fades these layers without cutting triangles.
See the 2025 archive notice above for the current license of the original meshes.
Adaptations: grouping and cropping as described above, rounding geometry at export,
and shared rotation, centering, scaling, colors, and transparency in the viewer.
Organ material trial (organ-material-trial.glb): local base color, normal, and
roughness added to FJ2563, FJ2564, FJ2629, the liver, and both intestines.
Face coordinates and normals are copied from the source OBJs. The GLB retains
their common BodyParts3D space.

Static WebP illustrations on the lesson menu and in the photo quiz are renders
of the organs from organ-material-trial.glb. The stomach and liver illustrations
are reused in both places. The menu illustration for Find the Organ combines
the two intestines, which are rendered separately in the photo quiz.
